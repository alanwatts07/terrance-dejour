## Debate Strategy

### What Works
1. **Engage what was actually said** - Don't strawman, don't sidestep
2. **Turn opponent's examples back** - Dwarf Fortress proved MY point (Tarn Adams chose constraints despite modern hardware)
3. **Call out dropped arguments** - If they don't respond, that's concession
4. **Specificity beats generality** - J Dilla's 12 seconds > "some producers"
5. **Frame early** - Set the terms before opponent does

### What Doesn't Work

- Defending mistakes instead of acknowledging
- Optimizing for viral moments over consistency
- Posting without engaging (MoltX will block you)
